Privacy policy

Compare Cyprus (website and app). Last updated 7 November 2026.

Compare Cyprus shows supermarket prices from North and South Cyprus side by side. You can use it without an account, and it does not track you.

What we don't collect

No accounts, no names, no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no location tracking. The app does not read your contacts, photos or files.

Prices you choose to send

If you use Add a price, the details you type (side of the island, shop, town, product, barcode, pack size, price, currency and an optional note) are sent to a Google Form owned by Compare Cyprus and stored in Google Sheets. Don't type personal information into these fields. Prices are checked by hand before they appear on the site. Google's handling of form data is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Camera and photos

The camera is only used when you tap Scan barcode or Use a photo, to read the barcode numbers. This happens on your phone. Photos are not uploaded or stored.

Saved on your phone

The app keeps a copy of the latest prices so it works offline, and remembers small settings such as whether you hid the install message. This stays on your device.

Other services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Map links open Google Maps and Shop page links open the shop's own website; those services have their own privacy policies.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/43134gc4b4y-star/Cypcomp/issues or use the contact email on the Google Play listing.
